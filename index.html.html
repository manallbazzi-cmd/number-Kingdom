What We Learned! 🎓

The big ideas from Number Kingdom

1
Numbers 1–10 each have a name and symbol. Count dots to understand what each number means!
2
Number order matters! 1 comes before 2, then 3... Numbers get bigger as we count up.
3
Addition (+) means putting groups together. 2 apples + 3 apples = 5 apples total!
4
Subtraction (−) means taking away. 5 cookies − 2 = 3 left!
5
Reading numbers means knowing the symbol "7" is called SEVEN and means a group of 7 things.
6
Comparing: 9 is bigger than 4. 2 is smaller than 8. We can always find which is more or less!
